## Maintenance

- Preserve the source register, revision history, and evidence classifications.
- Revalidate time-sensitive claims before operational or public use.
- Record new experiments separately with prompts, model/version information, and observations.
- Keep the master reference as the company, model-history, and general research hub; use the prompt-behavior synthesis for prompt findings and retain raw experiment records separately.
- Link to these references rather than duplicating substantive findings.
