# Research

**Purpose**

The research folder contains data, analysis, and decision support materials for studio operations.

This folder supports:
- Naming research and collision analysis
- Trend analysis
- Strategic planning
- Empirical decision-making

**Structure**

```
research/
├── ai-artists/          # AI-artist strategy and governance research
├── distribution/        # Distribution operations and platform research
├── naming/              # Naming research corpus
└── suno-platform/       # Consolidated Suno research
```

Platform-specific distribution references are organized under `distribution/platforms/`.

**Authority**

Research materials are informational and support decision-making by human editors. They do not create artists or bands; they inform naming strategy and collision risk.

**Governance**

- Research materials do not override manifesto or constitution
- Findings support but do not mandate decisions
- All research should cite sources
- Limitations and gaps should be documented
- Research references are not approved policy; preserve evidence classifications and revalidate time-sensitive platform claims before operational use
