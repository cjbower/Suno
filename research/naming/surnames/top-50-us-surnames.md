# U.S. Surname Research Status

**Status:** No validated surname ranking is maintained here.

The previous top-50 table, origin/meaning summaries, collision classifications, and cultural-branding recommendations are withdrawn. The source extraction and individual interpretations were not validated in this review. Do not rely on the former ranking or conclusions for naming, identity, audience, or availability decisions.

No current surname dataset was downloaded or reconstructed for this review. Future work should retrieve the source directly from the U.S. Census Bureau, record the release, coverage, definitions, transformations, and retrieval date, and avoid using surname frequency as a proxy for music-industry collision risk or cultural identity.
