# Surname Research

This folder is reserved for source-verified, aggregate surname research. The prior top-50 list and its origin, cultural, familiarity, and collision interpretations have been withdrawn; the underlying extraction was not validated in this review.

Surname frequency alone does not establish collision risk, searchability, cultural identity, language, audience, or suitability. Future work must cite an authoritative source, record its year and methodology, and keep source data separate from interpretation. Artist-name availability requires direct, dated searches and is not inferred from census frequency.

See the [Name Research Validation Report](../reports/name-research-validation-report.md), [Name Selection Strategy](../reports/name-selection-strategy.md), and [Official Baby-Name Data Source Review](../reports/official-name-data-source-review.md).
