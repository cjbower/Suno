# Reports

**Purpose**

This folder contains synthesis reports that extract insights from naming research corpus and provide recommendations for studio operations.

**Contents**

- name-selection-strategy.md – Principles and methodology for naming decisions
- name-trends-analysis.md – Generational patterns and insights
- name-research-validation-report.md – Completeness and gaps assessment

**Report Types**

**Strategy Reports** – Principles and frameworks
- Provide decision-making guidance
- Do not prescribe specific names
- Support editorial judgment

**Analysis Reports** – Synthesis and insights
- Draw conclusions from datasets
- Identify patterns and trends
- Document evidence

**Validation Reports** – Completeness and quality assessment
- Assess research corpus completeness
- Identify gaps and limitations
- Recommend future work

**How to Use**

1. Start with name-selection-strategy.md for principles
2. Review name-trends-analysis.md for generational context
3. Use name-research-validation-report.md to understand limitations
4. Cross-reference with specific datasets for detailed information

**Authority**

These reports support but do not mandate decisions. Editorial judgment remains authoritative.

**Governance**

Reports should:
- Cite supporting data
- Acknowledge limitations
- Avoid prescriptive language
- Support human decision-making
