# Naming Research Reports

This folder contains evidence-aware naming guidance. Reports support human review and do not approve or change artist, band, or project names.

## Reports

- [Name Selection Strategy](name-selection-strategy.md) - Human-led naming review principles.
- [Name Trends Analysis](name-trends-analysis.md) - Limits on historical name-frequency claims.
- [Official Baby-Name Data Source Review](official-name-data-source-review.md) - Evaluation of SSA, California CDPH, Washington DOH, and CDC/NCHS sources.
- [Name Research Validation Report](name-research-validation-report.md) - Current status and corrections to the prior corpus validation.

## Use

Review sources, retrieval dates, coverage, category labels, and limitations before citing a ranking. Aggregate name frequency does not establish audience familiarity, demographic identity, cultural fit, collision risk, or availability. Current collision checks require direct, dated searches.
