# Name Research Validation Report

**Review date:** 2026-11-25
**Status:** Structural inventory retained; source rankings, trend claims, and collision findings are not validated for operational use.

## Scope

This report corrects the earlier 2026-11-19 validation report, which described the naming corpus as comprehensive and asserted conclusions beyond the reproducible evidence retained in the repository.

The repository contains static first-name lists for the 1970s-2020s, a surname list, and prior artist/band naming assessments. The first-name lists do not preserve underlying annual counts or a reproducible extraction. The 2020s lists state a 2020-2024 window and are partial. No source dataset was refreshed in this review.

## Findings and Corrections

- The prior claim that observed list changes constitute the most significant generational naming shift in modern U.S. history is withdrawn.
- Claims that names signal age, audience familiarity, ethnicity, language, cultural fit, genre, or authenticity are withdrawn.
- Claims that a surname category provides a general searchability or collision advantage are withdrawn.
- Prior artist and band collision labels and recommendations are withdrawn. No live platform, domain, social, or trademark searches were performed as part of this review.
- Name popularity is not proof of artist-name availability, collision risk, audience preference, or suitability.
- Existing artist, band, and producer-project names were not changed or reassigned.

## Official Source Review

The [Official Baby-Name Data Source Review](official-name-data-source-review.md) evaluates SSA as the intended primary national source and California CDPH and Washington DOH as possible state supplements. The SSA page and data endpoints returned HTTP 403; the California data portal returned HTTP 502. The California data dictionary was accessible and confirms source-specific categories and name normalization/limitation details. Washington DOH's public page links to an interactive top-50 dashboard, but its complete coverage and extraction method were not verified.

No individual birth records were used, requested, or added. No new ranking data was downloaded or placed in the repository.

## Requirements for Future Validation

Before any name-frequency or collision conclusion is reused:

1. Reproduce the ranking from official aggregate counts and document exact years, geography, categories, inclusion rules, transformations, and ties.
2. Keep descriptive frequency separate from assumptions about people or audiences.
3. Use current, dated direct searches for music platforms, artist identifiers, domains, social accounts, and trademark records.
4. Record queries, sources, date, and limitations; do not treat absence from one source as proof of availability.
5. Obtain human approval for any canonical roster change. This report authorizes none.
