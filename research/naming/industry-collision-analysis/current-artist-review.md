# Current Artist Name Review – Collision Analysis

**Purpose**

This document assesses the current artist roster for industry collision risk, searchability challenges, and naming differentiation factors.

**Scope**

Review of 12 artists:
- Adrian Santiago
- Nathan Cross
- Isaac Vega
- Ethan Navarro
- Jordan Mercer
- Lucas Reed
- Elena Cruz
- Sophia Martinez
- Maya Bennett
- Isabella Reyes
- Natalie Vega
- Olivia Brooks

**Methodology**

For each artist:
1. Search music databases (Spotify, Apple Music, artist databases)
2. Document known matches with music industry professionals
3. Assess collision severity (Low/Medium/High)
4. Note distinguishing factors
5. Evaluate searchability

**Collision Classifications**

**Low Collision**
- Name is unique or rare in music industry
- No exact matches with notable artists
- Strong searchability potential
- Clear brand differentiation

**Medium Collision**
- Name shared by 1-3 music industry professionals
- Requires distinguishing factors (genre, description, visual branding)
- Moderate searchability with context
- Differentiation strategy needed

**High Collision**
- Name shared by multiple music industry professionals
- Exact matches with established artists
- Significant searchability disadvantage
- Strong marketing/branding differentiation required
