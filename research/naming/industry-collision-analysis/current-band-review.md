# Current Band Name Review – Collision Analysis

**Purpose**

This document assesses the current band roster for industry collision risk, searchability challenges, and naming differentiation factors.

**Scope**

Review of 6 bands:
- North Star Revival
- Lantern House
- Signal Fire
- Harbor Lights
- Midnight Avenue
- City Saints

**Methodology**

For each band:
1. Search music databases (Spotify, Apple Music, band databases)
2. Document known matches with music industry bands/groups
3. Assess collision severity (Low/Medium/High)
4. Note distinguishing factors
5. Evaluate searchability

**Collision Classifications**

**Low Collision**
- Name is unique or rare in music industry
- No exact matches with notable bands
- Strong searchability potential
- Clear brand differentiation

**Medium Collision**
- Name shared by 1-3 music industry bands/groups
- Requires distinguishing factors (genre, description, visual branding)
- Moderate searchability with context
- Differentiation strategy needed

**High Collision**
- Name shared by multiple music industry bands
- Exact matches with established or notable bands
- Significant searchability disadvantage
- Strong marketing/branding differentiation required
