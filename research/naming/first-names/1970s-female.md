# Top 20 Female First Names – 1970s

**Purpose**

This dataset documents the top 20 most popular female first names born during the 1970s in the United States.

**Source**

U.S. Social Security Administration – National Popular Baby Names Database (births 1970-1979)

**Methodology**

Ranked by frequency of occurrence in Social Security Administration birth records. Reflects names given to children born in the United States during this decade.

**Generational Branding Context**

These names reflect the naming preferences of parents born in the 1940s-1950s. Jennifer, Maria, and Mary dominate, showing preference for feminine traditional and biblical names. These names are strongly associated with Gen-X female cohort.

**Key Observations**

- Traditional and biblical names dominant
- Feminine endings (-er, -ly, -a, -y common)
- Three-syllable names more common than male equivalents
- Double-consonant endings popular (Sarah, Margaret, Ashley)

**Collision Risk**

- Jennifer, Maria, Mary: Very high collision
- Susan, Karen, Lisa, Nancy: Very high collision
- Moderate-to-high: Betty through Deborah

**Limitations**

- U.S. births only
- English-language preferences reflected
- Does not capture regional, ethnic, or cultural variations
- Reflects majority naming patterns; minority traditions under-represented
