# Top 20 Male First Names – 2020s

**Purpose**

This dataset documents the top 20 most popular male first names born during the 2020s in the United States (births 2020-2024).

**Source**

U.S. Social Security Administration – National Popular Baby Names Database (births 2020-2024)

**Methodology**

Ranked by frequency of occurrence in Social Security Administration birth records. Note: 2020s data is preliminary (only 4-5 years of data available).

**Generational Branding Context**

The 2020s continues the Gen-Z naming patterns established in 2010s. Liam, Noah, Oliver remain dominant. These names represent Gen-Alpha (born 2013-2024, though primarily 2020-2024 in this dataset).

**Key Observations**

- Liam remains #1 (consistent with 2010s)
- Top 20 is nearly identical to 2010s
- Naming preferences appear to be stabilizing in this new generation
- Contemporary/literary names continue to dominate
- Michael remains middle-of-pack (#12)

**Collision Risk**

Same as 2010s: Liam, Noah, Oliver carry HIGH collision risk in Gen-Z/Gen-Alpha context but LOW collision risk when searching across all generations.

**Data Quality Note**

2020s data is PRELIMINARY. Only 4-5 years of births recorded (2020-2024). Full decade data will not be available until 2029. This dataset should be considered provisional.

**Research References**

**Limitations**

- U.S. births only
- Preliminary data (incomplete decade)
- English-language preferences
- Regional and ethnic variations not captured
