**Purpose**

This dataset documents the top 20 most popular female first names born during the 1990s in the United States.

**Source**

U.S. Social Security Administration – National Popular Baby Names Database (births 1990-1999)

**Methodology**

Ranked by frequency of occurrence in Social Security Administration birth records.

**Generational Branding Context**

Female naming patterns remain remarkably stable across the 1970s-1990s period. Jennifer continues to dominate. These names represent Millennials (born 1990-2004).

**Key Observations**

- Jennifer continues as most common name by far
- Remarkable stability: Top 20 nearly identical to 1970s and 1980s
- Emily begins to rise in popularity (rank 13)
- Very slow generational change in female naming preferences

**Collision Risk**

Same as previous decades: Jennifer, Maria, Mary carry extremely high collision risk.
