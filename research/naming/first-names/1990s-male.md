# Top 20 Male First Names – 1990s

**Purpose**

This dataset documents the top 20 most popular male first names born during the 1990s in the United States.

**Source**

U.S. Social Security Administration – National Popular Baby Names Database (births 1990-1999)

**Methodology**

Ranked by frequency of occurrence in Social Security Administration birth records.

**Generational Branding Context**

The 1990s continues the dominance of traditional names. These names represent Millennials (born 1990-2004) and youngest Gen-X cohort.

**Key Observations**

- Traditional names continue to dominate
- Michael, Jason, Robert, James remain top 4
- Matthew and Daniel begin to rise in popularity (ranks 12, 11)
- Andrew enters top 20 (rank 19)

**Collision Risk**

Same as previous decades: Michael, Jason, Robert, James carry extreme collision risk.
