# Top 20 Female First Names – 2010s

**Purpose**

This dataset documents the top 20 most popular female first names born during the 2010s in the United States.

**Source**

U.S. Social Security Administration – National Popular Baby Names Database (births 2010-2019)

**Methodology**

Ranked by frequency of occurrence in Social Security Administration birth records.

**Generational Branding Context**

SIGNIFICANT SHIFT: Female naming shows radical departure from 70s-90s patterns. Jennifer, Maria, Mary (which dominated for 40 years) are COMPLETELY absent from top 20. Modern, literary, and contemporary names (Olivia, Emma, Ava) now dominate. These names represent Gen-Z (born 2010-2024).

**Key Observations**

- Olivia, Emma, Ava are NEW top names (completely absent from 1970s-2000s)
- Jennifer drops from #1 (1970s-2000s) to ABSENT (2010s)
- Maria and Mary completely absent from top 20
- Contemporary/literary names (Olivia, Scarlett, Harper) now dominate
- Short names popular (Mia, Ava, Ella)
- Some classical returns (Charlotte, Evelyn, Victoria, Elizabeth)

**Collision Risk**

DIFFERENT PATTERN: Olivia, Emma, Ava carry HIGH collision risk in Gen-Z context but VERY LOW collision risk in older generations. This is opposite of previous decades and creates unique branding advantage.

- U.S. births only
- English-language preferences
