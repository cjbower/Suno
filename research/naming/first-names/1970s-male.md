**Purpose**

This dataset documents the top 20 most popular male first names born during the 1970s in the United States.

**Source**

U.S. Social Security Administration – National Popular Baby Names Database (births 1970-1979)

**Methodology**

Ranked by frequency of occurrence in Social Security Administration birth records. Reflects names given to children born in the United States during this decade.

**Generational Branding Context**

These names reflect the naming preferences of parents born in the 1940s-1950s. The dominance of traditional, biblical, and classic names is notable. These names are strongly associated with Gen-X cohort (born 1965-1980).

**Key Observations**

- Traditional/classical names dominate
- Biblical names prominent
- Short, strong vowel endings common
- One-syllable or two-syllable names preferred

**Collision Risk**

- Michael, Jason, Robert, James: Very high collision (extremely common)
- John, David, William: Very high collision (classic names)
- Moderate-to-high: Christopher through Kevin

**Research References**

- U.S. Social Security Administration. Popular Baby Names. https://www.ssa.gov/oasis/

**Limitations**

- U.S. births only
- English-language preferences reflected
- Does not capture regional, ethnic, or cultural variations
- Reflects majority naming patterns; minority traditions under-represented
