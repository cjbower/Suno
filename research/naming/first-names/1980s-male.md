**Purpose**

This dataset documents the top 20 most popular male first names born during the 1980s in the United States.

**Source**

U.S. Social Security Administration – National Popular Baby Names Database (births 1980-1989)

**Methodology**

Ranked by frequency of occurrence in Social Security Administration birth records.

**Generational Branding Context**

The 1980s shows remarkable continuity with 1970s patterns. Traditional and biblical names remain dominant. These names are associated with elder Millennials and younger Gen-X (born 1980-1995).

**Key Observations**

- Extreme stability: Top names nearly identical to 1970s
- Traditional/classical names continue to dominate
- Biblical names remain prominent
- One-change from 1970s: Christopher rises to rank 10

**Collision Risk**

Same as 1970s: Michael, Jason, Robert, James carry extreme collision risk.
