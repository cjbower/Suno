# Top 20 Female First Names – 2000s

**Purpose**

This dataset documents the top 20 most popular female first names born during the 2000s in the United States.

**Source**

U.S. Social Security Administration – National Popular Baby Names Database (births 2000-2009)

**Methodology**

Ranked by frequency of occurrence in Social Security Administration birth records.

**Generational Branding Context**

Female naming patterns remain remarkably stable. Jennifer continues to dominate. These names represent Gen-Z (born 2000-2012) and younger Millennials.

**Key Observations**

- Jennifer continues top (consistent since 1970s)
- Top 20 remains identical to previous decades
- Emily continues to rise (rank 13 for three decades)
- Extremely slow change in female naming patterns

**Collision Risk**

Same as all previous decades: Jennifer, Maria, Mary carry extremely high collision risk.
