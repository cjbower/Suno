# Top 20 Female First Names – 1980s

**Purpose**

This dataset documents the top 20 most popular female first names born during the 1980s in the United States.

**Source**

U.S. Social Security Administration – National Popular Baby Names Database (births 1980-1989)

**Methodology**

Ranked by frequency of occurrence in Social Security Administration birth records.

**Generational Branding Context**

The 1980s shows remarkable continuity with 1970s patterns. Jennifer continues to dominate. These names are associated with Millennials (born 1981-1996).

**Key Observations**

- Extreme stability: Top 20 identical to 1970s
- Traditional/classical names continue dominant
- Jennifer remains by far the most common name
- Very gradual change in female naming preferences

**Collision Risk**

Same as 1970s: Jennifer, Maria, Mary carry extremely high collision risk.
