# Top 20 Male First Names – 2000s

> **Evidence notice:** Legacy static list; underlying counts and ranking method are not retained or validated. See the [Name Research Validation Report](../reports/name-research-validation-report.md).

**Purpose**

This dataset documents the top 20 most popular male first names born during the 2000s in the United States.

**Source**

U.S. Social Security Administration – National Popular Baby Names Database (births 2000-2009)

**Methodology**

Ranked by frequency of occurrence in Social Security Administration birth records.

**Generational Branding Context**

The 2000s continues the dominance of traditional names. These names represent Gen-Z (born 2000-2012) and younger Millennials.

**Key Observations**

- Michael, Jason, Robert, James remain top 4 (remarkably consistent since 1970s)
- Joshua enters top 20 (rank 19)
- Very slow change in male naming preferences
- Traditional/biblical names still dominant

**Collision Risk**

Extreme collision risk continues for Michael, Jason, Robert, James. Mid-range collision for Daniel through Andrew.
