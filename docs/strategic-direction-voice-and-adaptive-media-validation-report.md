## Adaptive Media Review

The chosen strategy is source-led and human-directed. A source may be considered for distinct companion formats only when the format serves its meaning and editorial purpose. A derivative has its own rights, contributors, format, provenance, review, and release decision; permission or approval for one medium does not automatically transfer to another.

Podcasts, publishing, video, educational material, and other accessible or interactive forms are examples for future assessment, not approved capabilities. Candidate formats remain unscheduled and require separate evidence and approval. The roadmap contains explicit categories for approved direction, unscheduled research/design candidates, and unapproved or inactive work.

The continuing source mission includes Psalms, Scripture, historical works, public-domain poetry, and other enduring cultural material. Self-sustainability is an approved goal; the broker/facilitator model is founder strategic interpretation, not an externally proven inevitability or market fact. Rapid-response and time-sensitive media remain strategic opportunities. Weekly sports and tailgate playlists are proposed use cases only, not launched products. Personalized and values-aware media remain proposed and would require privacy, consent, bias, copyright, accessibility, and safety controls. Podcasts, video shorts, and cross-platform derivatives require source lineage and rights review.

## Branded Distribution Strategy

Branded music distribution using wholesale or third-party delivery infrastructure is approved strategic direction, with direct digital-distributor or aggregator capability as the intended next maturity phase. The company intends to own the artist/client experience and internal operational functions while an evaluated partner supplies delivery where direct DSP relationships do not yet exist. Provider choice and all operational steps remain gated.

## Wholesale Infrastructure Boundary

The branded service is intended to control intake, validation, metadata, quality review, release preparation, catalog administration, reporting, and support as those functions are designed and verified. A wholesale, white-label, or delivery partner may handle DSP delivery. The eventual operating model must identify internal and provider-supplied functions and accurately disclose the provider where required. No provider has been selected, and no function is claimed complete merely because an interface or prototype exists.

## Three-to-Nine-Month Readiness Target

The branded-distribution target is a founder planning window of 3-9 months. It is not a promise of launch or commercial availability. Distinguish:

- **Internal readiness target:** Requirements, provider due diligence, architecture, and control design progress toward an internal alpha.
- **Pilot-launch target:** A very small private pilot may be considered within the window only after pilot-entry gates and explicit go/no-go approval pass.
- **Commercial availability target:** No date is set; it depends on evidence from the pilot and completed legal, financial, provider, security, support, and operational gates.
- **Full production capability:** Not established by a portal, internal alpha, or isolated successful delivery; it requires sustained evidence and a separate readiness decision.

## Direct Distribution Strategy

After establishing and operating the branded wholesale-backed service, pursue selective direct DSP relationships and direct digital-distributor or aggregator capability. Target functions include partnership management, production DDEX, release/resource metadata, territory/commercial data, acknowledgments, correction and takedown operations, identifier governance, rights representation, fraud prevention, royalty ingestion, statements and payouts, tax operations, support, security, continuity, and audit evidence.

## Twelve-to-Eighteen-Month Preferred Target

The preferred planning target for direct distributor or aggregator capability is 12-18 months. It is a target for staged readiness work, not a promise of acceptance or evidence that technical, operational, financial, legal, or platform gates are met.

## Less-Than-Two-Year Outer Target

The founder's outer planning target is less than 24 months. The date alone does not authorize application, partnership claims, customer commitments, or launch. Direct DSP approval remains an external dependency outside company control.

## External Dependencies

## Readiness Gates

The roadmap specifies five branded-distribution milestones: approved requirements and pilot scope; provider due diligence; internal-catalog alpha; a controlled private pilot with consented participants and explicit terms; and a service-readiness go/no-go after legal, financial, security, provider, migration, incident, and rollback controls pass.

Direct-distributor readiness is separate. Evidence must cover a controlled catalog, proven branded operations, low delivery/metadata error rates, rights/takedown controls, production DDEX, identifier governance, royalty accounting and timely reporting/payouts, tax/financial controls, security, monitoring, incident response, continuity, support, migration, exit strategy, and direct-partner relationship capability. Assess technical, operational, financial, legal, and platform acceptance separately. Do not handle client funds before the financial-control model has been reviewed and tested.

## Voice-Consent and Experiment Evidence

Consented real-person voice use is an approved future quality-control direction for assessing voice consistency, reproducibility, and drift; it is not an operational production workflow. The new consent governance requires explicit, informed, scope-specific authorization before capture, replication analysis, model creation, assignment, generation, editing, or distribution involving an identifiable real person's voice. Rights in the recording, composition, lyrics, likeness, and intended use require separate review. Authorization for one person, work, artist, provider, or medium is not blanket permission.

No sensitive consent evidence should be stored in Git by default. Future records may preserve minimal status and evidence references in an approved system; agreements and raw voice material require access-controlled storage and retention review.

`catalog/metadata/voices.yaml` was not changed and remains a placeholder registry. Voice-ID-001 remains partially validated experiment evidence: request/response metadata and human observations do not prove general identity consistency, production readiness, or consent for any artist assignment. No real voice assignment was made.
