# Archive

**Purpose:** Preserve historical, retired, and superseded materials from Suno Studio.

**Scope:** Retired artists, superseded prompts, rejected concepts, and other archived materials.

---

## Subdirectories

- **retired-artists/** – Artists no longer active in the studio
- **superseded-prompts/** – Prompts or workflows that have been replaced
- **rejected-concepts/** – Ideas or concepts that were not pursued
- **source-ingest/** – Original source documents retained for provenance and recovery

---

## Preservation Principle

Materials are archived to preserve historical context and decision rationale, not to delete or erase them.
