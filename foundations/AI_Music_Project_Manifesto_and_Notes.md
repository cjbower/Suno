# Project Notes: AI Music, Historical Storytelling, Psalms, and Artist Systems

## Origin of the Discussion

This document captures and organizes the ideas discussed regarding the use of AI music generation, particularly Suno, as a long-term creative platform rather than a novelty tool. The goal is fidelity to the discussion rather than invention. Where ideas were exploratory, they are presented as such.

A central theme emerged: the project is not primarily about generating individual songs. It is about building recognizable artistic identities, catalogs, audiences, and traditions that can evolve over time while remaining coherent.

## Initial Musical Exploration

The discussion began with creating music around Revolutionary War poetry. The working example was a poem about the capture of Carillon (Ticonderoga).

Several creative goals emerged:

- Preserve the original text as faithfully as possible.
- Introduce historical poetry to younger audiences.
- Avoid making the result sound like a history lecture.
- Avoid Broadway, musical theater, soundtrack music, or cinematic trailer music.
- Create something that could live in a modern playlist.
- Emphasize storytelling rather than spectacle.

The objective gradually evolved from creating a patriotic anthem into creating a modern record that happens to contain history.

## Production Direction Discovered Through Iteration

A number of preferences were identified through trial and error.

The desired music became:

- Dark electronic hip-hop.
- Strong low-end bass and controlled 808 impacts.
- Faster rap cadence and forward momentum.
- Narrative storytelling.
- Minimal vocal processing.
- Less singing and more rhythmic delivery.
- Instrumental breathing room between narrative sections.
- Strategic pauses and silence.
- Repeated phrases used sparingly to create hooks.

Several production elements were specifically rejected.

The project moved away from:

- Broadway aesthetics.
- Musical theater.
- Epic orchestra.
- Cinematic trailer music.
- Excessive vocal stacking.
- Choirs.
- Heavy reverb.
- Excessive delay.
- Artificial sounding vocals.
- Deep movie-trailer style narration.

A key realization was that the voice should feel like a believable narrator telling a story, not a performer trying to impress an audience.

## Chorus, Hooks, and Motifs

While studying the Ticonderoga poem, several candidate hook sections were discussed.

The strongest chorus candidate emerged as:

"And up springs the sun in his splendor..."

This stanza was identified as the emotional climax because it contains imagery, triumph, and emotional release without requiring modification of the poem.

A second motif involved:

"The old Green Mountain Lion, and his daring patriot band"

This was viewed less as a chorus and more as a recurring narrative motif.

The phrase:

"The chime of the silver bells"

was identified as an excellent closing image and potential recurring musical motif.

The eventual idea was not to make literal bells dominate the song. Instead, a subtle recurring metallic or glass-like musical figure could appear throughout the arrangement and become meaningful when the final lyric references the silver bells.

## Discoveries About Suno Prompting

A significant discovery was that over-specifying prompts often made results worse.

At various points Suno produced:

- Excessively deep voices.
- Robotic vocals.
- Gibberish.
- Instrumentals with missing lyrics.
- Over-produced performances.

The conclusion was that concise prompts often outperform highly detailed prompts.

The discussion evolved toward describing:

- The performer.
- The recording style.
- The storytelling approach.

rather than trying to describe every production detail.

Examples of preferred vocal characteristics included:

- Natural male vocal.
- Conversational delivery.
- Medium vocal range.
- Audible breaths.
- Close microphone.
- Minimal effects.
- Clear diction.
- Authentic performance.

## Clean Recording Philosophy

An important distinction emerged between musical style and recording style.

The desired recordings should feel:

- Clean.
- Close-mic.
- Minimally processed.
- Dry.
- Naturally performed.

Terms such as atmospheric, cinematic, ethereal, immersive, lush, and dreamlike were recognized as often leading Suno toward excessive sonic decoration.

Instead, preferred phrases included:

- Clean recording.
- Dry lead vocal.
- Minimal processing.
- Close vocal presence.
- Transparent compression.
- Natural room sound.
- Recorded performance aesthetic.

This became a guiding principle.

## Voice Consistency and Artist Identity

A major transition occurred when the discussion shifted from songs to artists.

Rather than creating isolated tracks, the idea emerged to create recurring creative identities.

Examples included:

- A recognizable vocalist.
- Consistent production DNA.
- Thematic continuity.
- Catalogs of work.
- Artist evolution over time.

The discussion compared this process to software architecture.

Instead of:

Song -> Song -> Song

The model became:

Artist -> Album -> Song

or even:

Label -> Artists -> Albums -> Songs

The recommendation was to maintain a permanent artist definition including:

- Voice characteristics.
- Production choices.
- Themes.
- Forbidden elements.

and then allow albums to evolve while remaining recognizably related.

## Producer Mindset versus Prompt Writer Mindset

A recurring theme was that many AI users create isolated outputs.

The proposed approach is more analogous to:

- A producer.
- A record label.
- An A&R executive.
- A catalog manager.

The goal becomes creating systems that reliably generate coherent work.

Potential assets include:

Artist Bible
Voice Definitions
Album Specifications
Song Release Tracking
Production Standards
Motif Libraries

The discussion compared this process to maintaining a platform or software product.

## Psalms Project

An entirely separate project emerged around the Psalms and daily readings.

The vision is to create recurring music based upon scriptural material.

Several principles were established.

The project is not intended to become generic worship music.

Instead the objective is:

- Meaningful listening.
- Contemporary production.
- Authentic artistry.
- Faithfulness to source material.
- Integration into daily life.

A listener might encounter the music:

- While commuting.
- During exercise.
- During reflection.
- As part of a weekly rhythm.

The intention is to release new material regularly rather than focusing on isolated major projects.

## Companion Podcast Concept

A companion podcast was proposed.

The relationship between podcast and music should be complementary.

Either:

- The podcast provides context for the music.

or:

- The music provides emotional reinforcement for the podcast.

Potential podcast content includes:

- Historical background.
- Scriptural context.
- Reflections.
- Explanations of source material.
- Storytelling.

A core observation was that music and podcasting can act as two ways of engaging the same ideas.

## Artist Stable Concept

One of the most important ideas in the discussion was the creation of a stable of recurring artists.

Rather than forcing all material into one style, different artists would naturally align with different source material.

Illustrative examples discussed included names such as:

- The Pilgrim.
- The Watchman.
- The Herald.
- The Chronicler.

These names were examples rather than finalized decisions.

The broader principle was that each artist should have:

- A distinct voice.
- Distinct production DNA.
- Distinct thematic tendencies.

A Psalm might fit one artist.

A Revolutionary War poem might fit another.

The source material should determine the assignment.

## Album Formation

Another key insight was that albums need not be planned first.

Instead:

Weekly releases accumulate.

Over time, songs naturally cluster around themes.

Then playlists or albums emerge from the catalog.

This was viewed as more natural and better aligned with modern listening habits.

Albums become curated collections of successful work rather than predetermined projects.

## Separation of Missions

The discussion concluded that two major creative initiatives should remain distinct.

### Initiative One: Psalms and Catholic Tradition

Purpose:

To bring scriptural and spiritual content into contemporary listening habits.

Potential content:

- Psalms.
- Daily readings.
- Sacred poetry.
- Themes of meaning, suffering, redemption, hope, beauty, and vocation.

### Initiative Two: History and Civic Memory

Purpose:

To bring historical stories, poetry, and civic virtues to life for modern audiences.

Potential content:

- Revolutionary War poetry.
- Historical speeches.
- Letters.
- Narratives.
- Themes of courage, sacrifice, duty, gratitude, and perseverance.

The conclusion was that these missions should remain cleanly separated even if they share production infrastructure.

## Audience

The target demographic discussed was approximately:

15-25 years old.

The purpose is not primarily persuasion.

A more accurate description may be cultural translation.

The goal is to help younger audiences encounter:

- Historical stories.
- Poetry.
- Scripture.
- Virtue-centered ideas.

through media habits that are already part of their lives.

## Motivation

An important statement made during the discussion was:

"I want to contribute to that legacy and keep the flame alive."

The project is not primarily motivated by wealth.

There was explicit acknowledgement that earning income is legitimate and necessary for family responsibilities.

However, the underlying motivation is cultural, educational, creative, and spiritual.

The aspiration is to extend the life of valuable stories, texts, virtues, traditions, and ideas by translating them into forms accessible to contemporary audiences.

## Working Conclusion

The central insight of the entire discussion is that the project is not fundamentally about AI-generated songs.

It is about creating a sustainable ecosystem of artists, catalogs, recordings, podcasts, stories, and traditions.

The songs are outputs.

The true creative work lies in designing a system capable of expressing meaningful ideas through recognizable artistic identities over many years.
