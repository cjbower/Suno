# Foundations

**Purpose:** Store the constitutional and founding documents that define Suno Studio's core identity, mission, and principles.

**Scope:** Foundational documents that should rarely change and represent the studio's highest-level commitments.

---

## What Are Foundations?

Foundations are the **constitutional basis** of Suno Studio. They answer the question: **Why does this studio exist, and what are the core principles it operates under?**

Foundations should be:
- Concise and memorable
- Rarely changed
- Authoritative for all creative decisions
- Traceable to documented decisions and reasoning

---

## Difference Between Foundations and Docs

### Foundations

Foundations are **constitutional** – they define principles that remain stable over long periods.

**Contents:**
- Manifestos (why the studio exists, vision, mission)
- Creative Constitution (core principles)
- Editorial Vision (founding perspective)
- Long-term Mission Statement
- Founding Philosophy

**Characteristics:**
- Foundational and rarely changed
- Apply to all work in the studio
- Answer "Why?" questions
- Set permanent direction

**Examples:**
- "Source material determines emotional objective" (principle)
- "Artist identity must remain recognizable" (principle)
- "Catalog continuity matters more than any individual song" (principle)

### Docs

Docs are **operational and procedural** – they document how the studio works, what decisions have been made, and what standards we follow.

**Contents:**
- Validation reports
- Decision logs (specific decisions and rationale)
- Operational standards
- Production guidelines
- Procedures and workflows
- Governance documentation

**Characteristics:**
- Specific to particular decisions or operational needs
- May change as the studio evolves
- Document "How?" and "What?" questions
- Provide transparency and traceability

**Examples:**
- "Decision to support 23 genres" (specific decision)
- "Production style guide preferences" (operational standard)
- "Release quality checkpoints" (operational procedure)

## Using These Documents

**When Creating or Planning Work:**
- Consult the Creative Constitution
- Refer to the Manifestos for strategic context
- Use these as the standard against which new initiatives should be evaluated

**When Making Decisions:**
- Document decisions in `docs/decision-log.md`
- Refer to Foundations if the decision changes or reinterprets a core principle
- Maintain traceability between decisions and foundational principles

**When Reviewing Work:**
- Verify alignment with Creative Constitution
- Check that Manifesto principles are being honored
- Ensure emotional authenticity and source material integrity
