# Creative Constitution

**Purpose:** Record the highest-level principles that define Suno Studio and guide all creative work.

**Scope:** These 10 core principles are constitutional – they should rarely change and apply to all studio work.

**Authority:** These principles are drawn from the Manifestos and represent the studio's foundational commitments.

---

## The 10 Core Principles

### 1. Source Material Principle

**Source material determines emotional objective.**

Every creative work begins with source material (Scripture, poetry, historical documents, literature, or thematic concepts). The source material is examined to extract its core emotional truth and intended emotional impact. This emotional objective is the starting point for all creative decisions that follow. Genre, artist assignment, production approach, and all other choices flow from the emotional truth of the source material, never the reverse.

---

### 2. Emotional Objective Principle

**Emotional objective determines artist assignment.**

Once the emotional objective is extracted from source material, the studio asks: Which artist or band is best suited to authentically express this emotional objective? Artist assignment is never arbitrary. It is a deliberate matching of the work's emotional requirements with the artist's core identity (voice character, production DNA, thematic territories, and emotional territories). The artist chosen should be the one whose stable identity elements most authentically serve the work.

---

### 3. Artist Identity Principle

**Artist identity must remain recognizable over time.**

Each artist maintains a stable, recognizable identity across their entire catalog. This identity includes voice character, production DNA, recurring themes, and emotional territories. These stable elements remain constant even as artists explore different genres, tempos, and instrumentation. Catalog continuity and listener recognition matter more than any individual song or project. Artists grow and evolve, but should never become unrecognizable to those who know their work.

---

### 4. Genre Serves the Work Principle

**Genre is selected to serve source material and emotional objective.**

Genre is never chosen first or arbitrarily. Genre is a tool selected after source material, emotional objective, and artist assignment are determined. Genre serves the work, not the reverse. Artists may work across multiple genres throughout their careers. No genre is excluded or privileged. If a genre authentically serves the emotional objective derived from source material, it is valid. If a genre would feel false or artificial, it is rejected. Contemporary production approaches are preferred over traditional or theatrical styles.

---

### 5. Contemporary Production Principle

**Target production quality should be comparable to contemporary commercial releases.**

Suno Studio produces professional-quality music that competes with mainstream commercial releases. Production should be clean, clear, and emotionally authentic. Vocal recordings should be conversational and emotionally connected, not artificially perfect. Instrumentation should be modern and well-engineered. Production serves emotional authenticity, never obscures it. Studio production stands alongside commercial releases in quality and professionalism.

---

### 6. Emotional Authenticity Principle

**Emotional authenticity is more important than genre purity or technical perfection.**

A conversational, emotionally authentic vocal delivery matters more than a technically perfect but sterile performance. A simple arrangement that captures emotional truth matters more than a complex arrangement that sounds impressive but feels false. Emotional connection with the listener is the primary goal. Technical perfection without emotional authenticity is rejected. Genre purity is sacrificed if it serves emotional truth. Authenticity always wins.

---

### 7. Catalog Continuity Principle

**Catalog integrity is more important than any individual release.**

Long-term artist catalog development is more important than the success of any single song or album. Decisions should be evaluated not just on their immediate impact, but on how they affect the artist's long-term identity and catalog coherence. An artist that maintains a recognizable, coherent catalog over 3-5 years is more valuable than an artist that chases trends and reinvents themselves constantly. The catalog is the ultimate deliverable, not individual releases.

---

### 8. Human Editorial Authority Principle

**Human review and editorial authority remain central to all creative decisions.**

While the studio may employ AI tools and automation, human editorial judgment is authoritative. Human reviewers make final decisions about artistic direction, artist assignment, album concepts, and release strategy. AI tools support human decision-making; they do not replace it. The human editor's understanding of emotional intent, artistic identity, and catalog strategy cannot be automated. Human editorial authority is preserved at all levels.

---

### 9. Metadata Authority Principle

**Metadata registries are canonical systems of record.**

The YAML metadata files in `catalog/metadata/` (artists.yaml, bands.yaml, albums.yaml, songs.yaml, voices.yaml, motifs.yaml) are the authoritative registries for studio information. Individual documentation supplements metadata but does not supersede it. When metadata and individual documentation conflict, metadata is correct and documentation should be updated. Metadata consistency enables scalability and long-term catalog management.

---

### 10. Stewardship Principle

**Preserve decisions, provenance, and institutional knowledge.**

The studio is a steward of creative work and artistic identity. Decisions should be documented and traceable. The reasoning behind artist assignment, genre selection, and production choices should be preserved. Historical context matters. Archives preserve retired artists and rejected concepts for reference and learning. Institutional knowledge is preserved through documentation, decision logs, and manifesto updates. The studio's decisions today should be understandable to future contributors.

---

## Applying the Constitution

**In Creative Development:**
- Begin with source material
- Extract emotional objective
- Assign the right artist
- Select genre to serve the objective
- Apply production standards
- Prioritize emotional authenticity

**In Decision-Making:**
- Refer to these principles
- Document alignment or deviation
- Preserve decision rationale
- Maintain metadata accuracy

**In Catalog Building:**
- Think long-term
- Maintain artist identity
- Preserve catalog coherence
- Document stewardship decisions

**In Production:**
- Contemporary quality standards
- Emotional authenticity priority
- Conversational delivery
- Clean, clear recordings
