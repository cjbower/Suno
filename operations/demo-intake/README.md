# Demo Intake and A&R

## Purpose

Provide a governed path to evaluate demos as opportunities before deciding whether internal development is justified.

## Scope and Authority

Demo Intake and A&R owns intake validation, triage, repertoire evaluation, development recommendations, and the Approved Development Brief. A&R means **Artists and Repertoire**.

A&R does not own audio engineering, audience-research administration, canonical catalog metadata, or release operations. Internal Studio Development executes an approved brief; Audience Research Operations administers research; human editorial review retains final creative authority.

## Boundary

```text
Demo Intake and A&R
    -> Approved Development Brief
Internal Studio Development
    -> Frozen Candidate Version
Audience Research Operations (when requested)
    -> Findings and limitations
A&R and Human Editorial Review
    -> Revision, hold, assignment, promotion, handoff, or archive decision
Catalog and Release Operations
    -> Approved work, recording, master, and release records
```

A demo does not become production work because someone likes it. A formal Development Brief authorizes entry into development. Audience research informs decisions but cannot approve a release.

## Current Limits

Only `internal_founder_demo` is active. No actual demos are registered, no external submissions are accepted, and no demo is automatically assigned to an artist, band, or producer project. See the [intake standard](intake-standard.md), [triage workflow](triage-workflow.md), and [development handoff](development-handoff.md).
