# Demo-to-Artist Mapping

## Possible Outcomes

A reviewed demo may be considered for:

- An existing solo artist
- An existing band
- An existing producer project
- A provisional new artist
- A provisional new band
- Unassigned repertoire work
- A voice candidate only
- A production reference only
- A song concept only

These are review outcomes, not current assignments. A demo may remain unassigned while preserving value.

## Studio Recommendations

The following are studio recommendations, not industry rules:

- One strong demo may create an identity candidate.
- Two compatible demos may justify provisional identity development.
- Three compatible and reproducible demos may justify consideration for active-roster promotion.

Compatibility and reproducibility require written human review. A threshold does not automatically create or promote an artist or band.

## Decision Evidence

Evaluate source and emotional fit, repeated creative characteristics, voice and production continuity, rights conditions, and reproducibility. State limitations and counter-evidence. Require an authorized human decision before any roster change.

No current demo is assigned to an entity in this phase.
