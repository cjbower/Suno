# Demo Evaluation Standard

## Grades

- **A – Exceptional:** Strong emotional response and clear development or release potential.
- **B – Strong:** Meaningfully above average and worth developing with identifiable improvements.
- **C – Average or Mixed:** Viable or interesting, but not yet compelling enough to prioritize without additional evidence.
- **D – Weak:** Substantial problems. Preserve only when one or more elements retain specific value.
- **F – Does Not Work:** Fails in its current form. Archive unless a salvageable voice, hook, production element, lyric, or concept is identified.
- **N/R – Not Rateable:** The reviewer lacked sufficient context, encountered a technical problem, or could not evaluate the criterion fairly.

## Application

- C is a valid midpoint, not a failure.
- Every grade requires written evidence.
- Overall grades are editorial judgments, not arithmetic averages.
- A demo with mixed grades may still contain a valuable asset.
- An inaccessible or incomplete demo is N/R, not F.
- Preserve strongest elements even when the overall disposition is decline or archive.
- Canonical review grades remain letters; do not encode them numerically without an approved future analysis specification.
