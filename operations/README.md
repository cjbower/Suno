# Operations

**Purpose:** Maintain operational guidance, workflows, and planning documents for Suno Studio.

**Scope:** Release planning, project workflow, automation opportunities, and strategic planning.

---

## Core Documents

- **roadmap.md** – Long-term strategic roadmap and planning
- **release-calendar.md** – Planned releases and timeline
- **workflow-notes.md** – Current workflow processes and documentation
- **automation-opportunities.md** – Potential automation and workflow improvements

---

## Operational Workflow

The studio workflow is conceptualized as:

```
Source Material
    ↓
Emotional Classification
    ↓
Artist Assignment
    ↓
Song Development
    ↓
Review
    ↓
Release
    ↓
Catalog
    ↓
Podcast Companion (if applicable)
```
