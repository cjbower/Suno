# Audience Research Governance

## Maturity Stages

### Stage 1 – Family Panel

Purpose: validate survey wording, test question comprehension and listening-session workflow, and identify obvious emotional or technical reactions.

Limitations: family participants know the creator and may share familiarity with the mission, source material, or desired outcome. Their feedback is usability and contextual evidence, not general market evidence. No family members are recruited or surveyed in this phase.

### Stage 2 – Friends and Invited Listeners

Purpose: broaden musical and demographic variety, include less directly involved listeners, and refine recruitment, privacy, and analysis practices.

Inactive. Before activation, define consent, anonymous identifiers, retention, survey versioning, cohort definitions, and approved handling of minors.

### Stage 3 – Curated Target-Audience Panel

Purpose: evaluate intended-listener response, compare genres, hooks, voices, and candidate versions, and test replay and discovery interest.

Inactive. Before activation, require participant consent, anonymous identifiers, a data-retention policy, survey versioning, cohort definitions, and approved handling of minors.

### Stage 4 – Public Audience Research

Purpose: broader preference testing, audience-segment comparison, and campaign or positioning research.

Inactive. Before activation, review privacy notice, consent, age and minor handling, data security, duplicate-response controls, public-link abuse, unreleased-audio redistribution, email collection, data export and deletion, and applicable legal and platform requirements.

## Authority

Audience Research Operations owns process integrity and findings. A&R and human editorial review decide what to do with evidence. Research does not approve creative direction, artist assignment, rights, technical acceptance, release, or catalog promotion. Marketing may consume findings under the boundaries in [cross-domain governance](../../docs/demo-and-audience-research-governance.md).

## No Activation

This architecture creates no participants, sessions, responses, surveys, recruitment, public links, or software accounts. No survey platform is selected.
