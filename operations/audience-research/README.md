# Audience Research Operations

## Purpose

Provide a governed way to collect and report structured listener evidence for A&R, creative development, positioning, and marketing.

## Authority Boundary

Audience Research Operations owns participant process, survey administration, instrument versioning, session integrity, privacy handling, analysis, and reporting. It does not own artist assignment, rights approval, technical acceptance, final creative revision, release approval, or catalog promotion.

Research informs A&R and human editorial review; it does not replace artistic judgment, source fidelity, rights review, technical validation, or human authority. Marketing may consume approved findings but does not control the research record or alter findings.

## Maturity

The proposed maturity path begins with a family panel to test survey clarity and workflow, then may expand to friends and invited listeners, a curated target-audience panel, and public research. Only the design for Stage 1 is documented as the initial stage. No stage is active in this phase: no participants are recruited, no survey is sent, and no participant data is created.

Before any later stage, review the requirements in [governance](governance.md), [participant cohorts](participant-cohorts.md), and [privacy and retention](privacy-and-retention.md).

## Records and Limitations

Schema examples are in `catalog/metadata/audience-research-sessions.yaml` and `catalog/metadata/audience-responses.yaml`. They contain no sessions or responses. Future production use may require an approved database or survey platform; no platform is selected here. Do not store sensitive participant responses in Git by default.
