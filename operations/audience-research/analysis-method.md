# Audience Research Analysis Method

## Required Report Contents

- Response count
- Grade distribution
- Median grade
- N/R count
- Cohort breakdown
- Strong agreement
- Strong disagreement
- Repeated written themes
- Exact candidate version evaluated
- Survey version
- Session limitations

## Analysis Rules

- Do not create automatic pass or fail thresholds.
- Do not hide polarized results behind one average.
- Report distributions and disagreement, not only a central statistic.
- Do not merge family, friends, target-audience participants, musicians, producers, or technical reviewers without preserving cohort distinctions.
- Treat grades as ordinal categories; any numeric encoding requires a separately approved analysis specification.
- Separate observed response from interpretation and recommendation.
- Suppress or combine small groups when reporting could identify a participant.
- Human A&R and editorial review decide whether findings change development.
