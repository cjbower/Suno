# Audience Research Evaluation Standard

## Letter Grades

Use A, B, C, D, F, or N/R. Retain the descriptive labels in the instrument and in reporting; do not display a numeric score in their place.

- **A:** Exceptional response on the stated criterion.
- **B:** Strong response with identifiable appeal.
- **C:** Mixed or midpoint response; not a failure.
- **D:** Weak response on the stated criterion.
- **F:** Does not work for the respondent on the stated criterion.
- **N/R:** Not rateable because the respondent lacked context or a technical or accessibility issue prevented fair evaluation.

These are descriptive categories, not pass/fail thresholds. Do not convert them to a canonical numerical score.

## Interpretation

- Preserve the middle position unless a specific future study approves a different design.
- Report the exact candidate and survey versions evaluated.
- Do not treat stated intent as observed behavior.
- Do not present audience ratings as artistic approval or release authorization.
- Retain cohort distinctions and disclose study limitations.
