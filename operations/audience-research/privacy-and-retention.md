# Privacy and Retention

## Data Minimization

Collect only information needed for an approved research question. Prefer anonymous participant IDs and optional age bands; never collect names, email addresses, birth dates, or direct contact details in the research response schema.

Pseudonymous identifiers and free-text answers may still be identifiable. Review open responses for names or other identifying details before storage or sharing. Keep any future linkage key separate, access-controlled, and time-limited.

## Consent and Minors

No participant is recruited and no consent is collected in this phase. Before any future session, define consent, notice, withdrawal, age screening, and approved handling of minors. Do not include minors until a reviewed process and required approvals are in place.

## Sensitive Cohorts and Responses

Some cohort labels or open responses may reveal sensitive beliefs or other personal information. Participation and sensitive disclosures must be voluntary and limited to a necessary purpose. Avoid small-cell reporting and do not infer sensitive traits.

## Storage, Access, and Retention

Do not store future sensitive individual responses in Git by default. Before activating research, approve the storage platform, access roles, retention period, export controls, correction/deletion process, security measures, and incident escalation. No platform is selected here.

Protect unreleased audio from unauthorized access, downloads, redistribution, or public-link abuse. Audio access must be limited to the approved study and removed according to the retention policy.

## Current State

No participant records, survey responses, session data, consent records, or audio copies were created by this phase.
