# Participant Cohorts

## Future Cohort Values

- `family`
- `friend`
- `musician`
- `producer`
- `faith_community`
- `history_interest`
- `general_listener`
- `target_age`
- `outside_target_age`
- `subject_matter_reviewer`
- `technical_reviewer`

These are schema design values, not participant records or recruitment targets.

## Interpretation

- Keep cohorts distinguishable in collection and analysis.
- Do not merge cohorts without explicit disclosure and rationale.
- Family feedback is valuable for comprehension and workflow testing but is not representative market evidence.
- Musicians, producers, subject-matter reviewers, technical reviewers, and audience listeners answer different questions; report their roles separately.
- `target_age` and `outside_target_age` refer to approved age bands, never birth dates or exact ages.
- `faith_community` may reveal sensitive beliefs. Use only if necessary, voluntary, and approved; do not infer it or connect it to identity. Avoid reporting small groups that could identify participants.

## Participant Identifiers

Future sessions should use anonymous participant identifiers. A code is not proof of anonymity if it can be linked back to a person. Do not create participant records or collect direct identifiers in this phase.
