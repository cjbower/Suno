# Internal Demos

## Purpose and Scope

Use an internal-only workflow for demos created or controlled by the studio. The only active submission class is `internal_founder_demo`. One intake-incomplete record is registered; additional founder demos are pending submission.

## Ownership Boundary

Intake metadata records provenance and an ownership assertion, not a legal conclusion. Source-material rights, translations, samples, provenance, and generator rights still require review. The original demo remains immutable.

## Authoritative Metadata

Use the canonical registry in [`../../metadata/demos.yaml`](../../metadata/demos.yaml). Queue folders are organizational locations, not registries. Status, disposition, and lineage belong in metadata.

## Lifecycle Locations

- `intake/` – received internally; completeness not yet established
- `under-review/` – intake validated and under triage or A&R review
- `approved-seeds/` – approved as a development reference, not yet a work record
- `development/` – development authorized by a formal Development Brief
- `on-hold/` – preserved pending a named review trigger
- `archived/` – retained without current action

## Asset Distinctions and Limitations

A demo is not automatically a work, recording, master, or release. Promotion creates separate records and preserves source lineage. The first record references a Suno page but has no locally preserved audio; no artist assignments or development authorizations are present.

## Future Considerations

Future internal intake may include roster-artist, band, producer-project, collaboration, acquired-concept, inbound-pitch, or organic-discovery classes. Those classes are not active merely because the schema anticipates them.
