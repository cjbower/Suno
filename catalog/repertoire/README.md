# Repertoire

**Purpose:** Organize and document source material that informs Suno Studio creative work.

**Scope:** Scripture, poetry, historical documents, and other material sources used as the foundation for songs and projects.

---

## Structure

- **psalms/** – Psalms as source material for music projects
- **daily-readings/** – Daily Scripture readings and meditations
- **revolutionary-war/** – American Revolutionary War documents and narratives
- **historical-documents/** – Historical texts and primary sources
- **virtues/** – Virtue literature and character-focused material
- **saints/** – Saints' lives, hagiography, and spiritual narratives
- **poetry/** – Poetry and literary material

---

## Organization Principle

Each subdirectory contains:
- `README.md` – Scope and purpose
- Document files – Individual source materials
- Metadata – Cross-references to related songs or projects

---

## Cross-Reference to Creative Work

Source materials are linked to created works through:
- Song metadata in `../metadata/songs.yaml`
- Album themes in `../albums/*/theme.md`
- Artist themes in `artist-roster/solo-artists/*/themes.md`
