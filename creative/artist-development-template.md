# Artist Development Template

**Purpose:** Define the structural framework and guidance for developing, documenting, and evolving solo artist identities within Suno Studio.

**Scope:** This template applies only to solo artists. Use it to maintain consistency and ensure that artist identity remains stable and recognizable across all projects and collaborations.

---

## Document Structure

Each solo artist folder in `artist-roster/solo-artists/` contains the following core documents:

1. **artist-profile.md** – Public-facing identity and positioning
2. **artist-development-plan.md** – Strategic direction and evolution
3. **production-dna.md** – Technical and stylistic foundations
4. **themes.md** – Core thematic territories and recurring emotional territories
5. **discography.md** – Complete song and project listing
6. **album-history.md** – Album participation and project history

## Implementation Notes

- **Never invent personal history.** Restrict biographical details to what has been explicitly established through studio work.
- **Use placeholders liberally.** It is better to have a structured empty field than to fabricate information.
- **Link to canonical metadata.** Artist profiles should cross-reference canonical metadata files in `catalog/metadata/artists.yaml`.
- **Document decisions, not assumptions.** If a characteristic has been chosen for the artist, document the reason or source material that led to that choice.
- **Preserve consistency.** Any change to core identity elements should be documented and tracked in the artist development plan.
