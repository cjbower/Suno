# Genre Portfolio

**Purpose:** Document the complete range of musical genres supported by Suno Studio, with industry context and guidance on when each genre may serve studio projects.

**Scope:** This is a reference guide for genre selection and artist assignment. No artist is restricted to a single genre. No genre is excluded or privileged. Genre is selected based on source material and emotional objective, not artist preference or convention.

---

## Core Principle

Suno Studio is **not genre-constrained**.

Every supported genre is a tool for expressing emotional objectives derived from source material. Genre is selected to serve the work, not to constrain it. If a genre authentically serves the source material and emotional objective, it is valid. If it would feel false, it is rejected.

## Bilingual and Language Considerations

Suno Studio welcomes:

- **Spanish-language content** – from Latin American, Mexican, and Spanish traditions
- **Bilingual content** – blending Spanish and English
- **Other language content** – based on source material and artist identity
- **Language as artistic choice** – language selection serves source material and emotional objective

## Genre Evolution

Artists may work across multiple genres throughout their careers. Genre evolution is natural and expected when:

- Source material and emotional objective call for a different genre
- Artist identity remains stable (voice, production DNA, themes)
- Genre change is intentional and documented
- Evolution remains authentic to the artist

---

## Exclusions

No genre is excluded from Suno Studio based on:

- Commercial viability
- Production trends
- Label preference
- Personal taste

All genres are equally valid tools for expressing emotional truth derived from source material.
