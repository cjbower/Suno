# Vocal Style Guide

**Purpose:** Define vocal recording, performance, and production standards for Suno Studio.

**Scope:** Applies to all vocal recording unless artist-specific production DNA specifies otherwise.

---

## Vocal Performance Standards

### Delivery

- **Conversational First:** Vocal delivery should be conversational and natural before technically perfect
- **Emotional Authenticity:** Emotional truth and connection matter more than technical perfection
- **Dynamic Performance:** Performance should include dynamic range and variation in intensity and tone
- **Minimal Processing:** Preserve the artist's voice character

### Expression

- **Avoid Vocal Affectation:** Don't adopt artificial vocal styles or accents
- **Avoid Oversinging:** Don't add unnecessary embellishment or runs
- **Avoid Vocal Gymnastics:** Technically impressive melismas are often out of character
- **Prioritize Clarity:** Vocal clarity and lyrical intelligibility should be maintained

---

## Vocal Recording Process

### Pre-Recording

- Vocal warm-up appropriate to artist's voice
- Full performance rehearsal to establish performance feel
- Multiple takes to capture authentic emotion

### Recording

- **Proximity:** Artist position relative to microphone should feel natural and comfortable
- **Breath:** Natural breathing and breath sounds may be preserved if authentic to performance
- **Takes:** Multiple takes allow selection of best performance, not best technical execution
- **Live vs. Comped:** Consider whether live full take or comped vocal serves emotional objective

### Processing

**Minimal Use of Effects:** Default to minimal processing. Effects should enhance, not create.

- **Compression:** Use to control dynamics without destroying natural feel
- **EQ:** Use to enhance clarity without artificiality
- **Reverb:** Minimal; preserve sense of space without washing out voice
- **Delay:** Minimal; use if it serves the arrangement

---

## Vocal Production Philosophy

### Preferred Characteristics

- Clean and clear vocal recordings
- Voice character remains recognizable
- Dynamic range preserved
- Emotional connection maintained
- Contemporary production standard

### Avoid

- Excessive vocal processing
- Autotuning or pitch correction that destroys natural feel
- Vocal stacking that obscures primary vocal
- Over-compressed vocals that sound "squashed"
- Reverb-heavy vocals that lack clarity

---

## Multiple Vocal Takes and Comping

**Selection Over Perfection:** Emphasize selecting the take with best emotional performance, not best technical execution.

**Comp Carefully:** If comping multiple takes, maintain consistency of performance feel.

**Preserve Character:** Comping should enhance, not change the artist's vocal character.

---

## Harmony and Layered Vocals

**Layering Principle:** Layered vocals should enhance, not bury, the primary vocal.

**Harmony Balance:** Harmonies should be clear and serve the arrangement without competing with lead.

**Doubling:** Doubles can reinforce the lead vocal but should maintain primary vocal's clarity and character.
