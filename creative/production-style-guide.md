# Production Style Guide

**Purpose:** Define general production standards, preferences, and best practices for Suno Studio.

**Scope:** Applies to all original music production unless artist-specific production DNA specifies otherwise.

---

## Preferred Production Characteristics

Suno Studio prioritizes:

- **Clean Recordings** – Well-engineered, clear vocal and instrumental recordings
- **Conversational Vocals** – Natural delivery and emotional authenticity over theatrical or artificial performance
- **Minimal Vocal Processing** – Preserve voice character without excessive effects or compression
- **Strong Emotional Connection** – Production choices prioritize emotional impact and authenticity
- **Modern Production** – Contemporary production techniques and mixing standards
- **Dynamic Contrast** – Dynamic range and variation in production texture and energy

---

## Production Approaches to Avoid

Do not use:

- **Excessive Reverb** – Reverb should not cloud or obscure vocal clarity
- **Excessive Delay** – Delay effects should enhance, not dominate
- **Broadway or Musical Theater Vocal Style** – Theatrical delivery is out of character for studio aesthetic
- **Overproduced Vocals** – Excessive processing destroys emotional authenticity
- **Robotic Vocals** – Artificial or synthesized vocal delivery should be intentional, not accidental
